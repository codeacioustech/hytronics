Custom Magnetics Inductors, Transformers & Common Mode Chokes

For over 40 years Hytronics Corp. has designed and manufactured custom magnetics and transformers for OEM customers in the commercial, industrial, and telecommunications fields. Our expertise lies in manufacturing custom transformers and custom inductors designed to meet our customers’ specific needs in the area of toroidal transformers, inductors, common mode chokes, and air coils, as well as power magnetics.

We pride ourselves in spending the time to work with the customer upfront in the design stage to ensure the custom magnetics design will not just meet their needs, but exceeds them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Hytronics Corp. manufacture?

Hytronics designs and manufactures custom magnetics including transformers, inductors, common mode chokes, air coils, and power magnetics for commercial, industrial, military, aerospace, and telecommunications customers.

What military standards does Hytronics support?

Hytronics supports MIL-STD-1553 transformers and magnetics, MIL-PRF-21038 standard magnetics, and MIL-PRF-27 custom magnetics. The company is ISO 9001:2015 registered with manufacturing in the US and Costa Rica.

What is Hytronics Cage Code?

Hytronics Corp. Cage Code is 29525. The company was established in 1967.

About the Company

Hytronics designs and manufacturers Common Mode Chokes, Toroidal Transformer, Low Profile Transformer & Planar Transformer for Military and Industrial customers.
